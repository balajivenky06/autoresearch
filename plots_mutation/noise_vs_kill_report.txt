==============================================================================
 INTERPRETATION
==============================================================================
  avg_noise_rate is identically 0.0 across every RAG cell.
  Our knowledge base never returns chunks below the cosine<0.3
  threshold, so the noise-rate signal is degenerate. This is a
  finding in itself — the testing-docs KB is well-curated
  relative to the per-task queries — but it means noise rate
  cannot be correlated with anything in this dataset.

  avg_faithfulness measures token overlap between the generated
  tests and the retrieved context (RQ3). A positive correlation
  with mean_kill_rate would say: tests that lift more vocabulary
  from the retrieved docs are also better at killing mutants.

  avg_llm_judge_faithfulness is the DeepSeek-Coder 6.7B judgement
  of how grounded the tests are in the retrieved context.
  Validated Pearson r=0.925 vs human in the docstring companion
  paper, so it's a higher-fidelity grounding signal than the
  token-overlap proxy.
